# Curated Further-Reading Library

This library groups selected sources by the course chapters. It does not claim to cover the entire internet. It prioritizes primary technical documentation, established pattern catalogs, influential authors, published company case studies, and Persian learning resources. These links support and extend the lessons; the course itself is designed to teach the core concepts without requiring outside reading.

As you read, ask: What problem and constraints did the source describe? Which options were compared? At what scale was the result reported? What costs or side effects came with it? Is the author reporting a case study or claiming a universal rule? Do not copy a company’s experience without comparing its workload, teams, and constraints.

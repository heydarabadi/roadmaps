## What you will learn

- Describe a pattern through its problem, context, forces, solution, and consequences.
- Recognize the 23 Gang of Four patterns and compare their creational, structural, and behavioral roles.
- Explain how POSA patterns address broader system forces such as concurrency, distribution, and communication.
- Compare a patterned design with a direct implementation and account for abstraction, coupling, and maintenance costs.

## How to study this chapter

For each pattern, first state the recurring problem and the forces that make it difficult. Then identify its participants and collaboration, and evaluate the consequences. Reimplement a small example without the pattern before deciding whether the extra vocabulary or indirection helps. A familiar pattern name does not prove that its structure fits your code.

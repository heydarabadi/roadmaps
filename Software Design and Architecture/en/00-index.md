## How to use the course

Read the integrated lesson first, then use the standalone topic page for focused review, examples, and a topic quiz. Quizzes include explanatory answers. Open-ended exercises offer sample reasoning rather than a single correct answer. Evaluate design choices against the problem, constraints, and costs; record important decisions as ADRs.

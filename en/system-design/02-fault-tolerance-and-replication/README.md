# Chapter 02 — Fault Tolerance and Replication

[فارسی](../../../fa/system-design/02-fault-tolerance-and-replication/README.md)

This chapter examines how systems detect failed components, shift traffic or leadership, and keep copies of data in sync. It focuses on failover, replica roles, lag, recovery, and the limits of redundancy.

## Topics

- [Failover](08-failover/README.md)
- [Replication](09-replication/README.md)

## Learning outcome

You should be able to distinguish failover from replication, choose a recovery objective, and reason about stale reads, split brain, and common-cause failures.
